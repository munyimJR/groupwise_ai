# Interview guide (15 minutes, 5–8 people)

Goal: hear **real stories** behind the survey numbers. Talk to students from different group types: a flat, a
friend group that eats out, a trip group and a club. One person asks, one person writes notes.

**Before you start (read aloud):** "We're students building a hackathon project about how groups share money.
There are no right answers. We won't record your name, and we'll only quote you without names. Is that OK?"
Do not record audio unless the person agrees.

## Questions

1. **Last time.** Tell me about the last time you paid for something for a group. What was it, who was there,
   and how did everyone pay you back?
2. **Tracking.** How did you know who owed what? What went wrong, if anything?
3. **Waiting.** How long did it take to get the money back? How did that feel? Did you have to remind anyone?
4. **Wallet.** Which app did you use to send or receive the money? Did you use any "request money" feature?
5. **Fairness.** Is there someone in your group who always pays first? How does the group feel about that?
6. **Spending.** Do you know what your group spends most on? Has anyone ever been surprised by the total?
7. **Saving together.** Has your group saved for a trip or event? How did you collect the money? Did it work?
8. **Show the prototype** (2 minutes: dashboard → wallet import → settle up → goal). What would you use? What
   is confusing? Would you trust it with your wallet data?
9. **Inside the wallet.** Would you rather have this inside the wallet app you already use, or as a separate app? Why?

## Afterwards

Put every pain point on a sticky note (or a row in a sheet) and group similar ones. Count how many people
mention each theme. Pick 2–3 short anonymous quotes for the report and the deck.
